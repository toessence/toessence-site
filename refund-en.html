Refund Policy

Updated: June 2026

We want you to be satisfied with toEssence. If the subscription did not meet your needs, you may request a refund under the conditions below.

1. Request Evaluation & Conditions

You may request a refund by contacting our support team. We review each request individually and, where justified, process the refund within 14 days of your request.

We reserve the right to decline a refund or issue a partial refund, particularly if:

  • More than 14 days have passed since the payment date;
  • the paid plan has already been substantially used (daily filter limits were exhausted frequently during the paid period);
  • there are clear signs of service abuse, automated script usage, or limit circumvention;
  • a refund has already been issued for this specific personal license (UUID or email) in the past.

2. How to Request a Refund

Users cannot issue or request a refund directly through the Paddle interface. To request a refund, you must follow these steps:

  • Cancel your subscription via the subscription management page in the extension settings (this stops future recurring charges).
  • Contact us via email at hello@toessence.net or through our website's contact form. Please provide the exact email address used for checkout and the transaction date.

Our team will review your request within 2–3 business days, locate your payment ID, and manually process the refund via our merchant dashboard.

3. Refund Processing

All transactions are handled by our partner Paddle.com (Merchant of Record). Once we manually approve and issue the refund from our end, Paddle processes the payout back to the exact same payment method used for the initial purchase. Depending on your financial institution, the funds usually take 2 to 7 business days to appear on your account.

4. Subscription Renewal and Cancellation Rules

Subscriptions operate on a recurring billing cycle and renew automatically each month. You can cancel your subscription at any time. Please note: upon cancellation, your access to premium features and higher filter limits under your personal license remains active until the end of the current billing month (your plan does not terminate instantly, it simply won't renew for the next cycle).

Charges for an already activated or automatically renewed billing period are non-refundable if the user simply forgot to cancel in time and continued to utilize the Service limits.

5. Contact Us

For any inquiries regarding refunds, cancellations, or billing issues, please contact us at: hello@toessence.net
