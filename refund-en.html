Refund Policy

Updated: June 2026

We want you to be satisfied with toEssence. If the subscription did not meet your needs, you may request a refund under the conditions below. This Policy applies to paid subscriptions (Easy, Pro); the Free plan involves no payment.

1. Request Evaluation & Conditions

You may request a refund by contacting our support team. We review each request individually in the user's interest. Your request will be reviewed and a refund decision made within 5 business days of your request.

We reserve the right to decline a refund or issue a partial refund, particularly if:

  • more than 14 days have passed since the payment date;
  • the paid plan has already been substantially used (daily filter limits were exhausted frequently during the paid period);
  • there are clear signs of service abuse, spam, automated requests, or limit circumvention;
  • a refund has already been issued for this specific personal license (UUID or email) in the past.

This Policy does not limit your mandatory consumer rights under applicable law (including, where provided, the right of withdrawal within 14 days). In case of conflict, mandatory statutory provisions prevail.

2. How to Request a Refund

Users cannot issue a refund themselves through the Paddle interface. To request a refund, follow these steps:

  1. Cancel your subscription via the subscription management page in the extension settings (this stops future recurring charges).
  2. Contact us via email at hello@toessence.net or through our website's contact form. Please provide the email address used for checkout and the transaction date.

We will review your request, locate the transaction in our records, and initiate the refund manually.

Please contact our support first. If, instead of contacting us, you initiate a payment dispute (chargeback) with your bank or with Paddle, we reserve the right to suspend or deactivate the corresponding license until the dispute is resolved.

3. Refund Processing

All transactions are handled by our partner Paddle.com (Merchant of Record). Once we approve your request, Paddle sends the refund exclusively to the same payment method (card or wallet) used for the initial purchase.

Depending on your bank, the funds usually take 2 to 7 business days to appear on your account. The Service is not responsible for any additional fees or exchange-rate differences that may arise from your bank when the funds are credited.

4. Subscription Renewal and Cancellation Rules

Subscriptions operate on a recurring billing cycle and renew automatically each month. You can cancel your subscription at any time. Upon cancellation, your access to premium features and higher filter limits under your personal license remains active until the end of the current billing month (your plan does not terminate instantly, it simply won't renew for the next cycle).

Charges for an automatically renewed billing period are non-refundable if the user forgot to cancel in time and made at least one request (used a filter) in the new billing period.

5. Contact Us

For any inquiries regarding refunds, cancellations, or billing issues, please contact us at: hello@toessence.net
