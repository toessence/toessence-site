Refund Policy

Updated: June 2026

We want you to be satisfied with toEssence. If the subscription did not meet your needs, you may request a refund under the conditions below.

1. Reviewing Your Request

You may request a refund by contacting us (see below). We review each request individually and, where justified, issue a refund within 14 days of your request.

We may decline a refund or issue a partial refund, in particular if: the plan has already been substantially used (daily limits for the paid period exhausted); there are signs of abuse or limit circumvention; a refund has already been issued for this account before; or a significant amount of time has passed since payment.

2. How to Request a Refund

To request a refund, contact us using one of the following: email hello@toessence.net or the contact form on our website. Please include the email you used for payment and the payment date. We will review your request within a few business days.

3. Refund Processing

Payments are processed by our partner Paddle.com, which acts as the Merchant of Record. Approved refunds are issued through Paddle to the same payment method used for the purchase. It may take a few business days for the funds to appear, depending on your bank or payment provider.

4. Subscription Renewal

The subscription renews automatically each month. To avoid being charged for the next period, cancel your subscription before the renewal date via the subscription management page. Charges for a period already used are generally non-refundable.

5. Contact

For any questions about refunds, contact us at: hello@toessence.net
