Project Mission

toEssence is a browser extension designed as a tool for cognitive security and digital hygiene. Its functionality addresses the problem of information overload caused by the continuous flow of low-quality and manipulative content online.

The system algorithm automatically filters incoming text, removing clickbait, emotional triggers and hidden manipulations, then generates a concise and structured summary within seconds.

This approach automates the process of initial information verification. Instead of passively consuming large volumes of texts and videos, users get the key points in seconds — helping reclaim control over their time, reduce mental fatigue and maintain focus on professional tasks.
