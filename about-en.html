Project Mission

toEssence is a browser extension created to support your digital hygiene and mental well-being. It helps solve the problem of information overload caused by the endless stream of low-quality, manipulative content online.

The extension automatically filters the text, clearing away clickbait, emotional triggers, and hidden manipulation, and leaves you with a clean, structured summary in just a few seconds.

This completely changes how you interact with information. Instead of passively spending time on long articles, you get the main points instantly. It's a simple way to take back control of your time, reduce digital fatigue, and keep your mind clear for what truly matters to you.
