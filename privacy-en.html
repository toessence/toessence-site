Privacy Policy

Updated: May 2026

toEssence is built with a Privacy by Design approach. Data collection is minimized and information is never sold to third parties.

1. What data we collect

On the Free plan:

  • Anonymous UUID — generated locally in your browser to identify the session.
  • Country & region — determined automatically from a non-stored IP for correct local pricing.
  • Filter type — anonymized data about content type (news, YouTube, reviews).
  • Timestamp — date and time of the request.
  • Page URL & text fragment — when you hover a headline, the page URL and a fragment of the article/subtitles are sent to our server to generate the summary. The URL and the generated summary are cached temporarily; the source text is processed and not retained after the summary is created.

We strictly do NOT collect: your name, phone number, or exact IP address (only an irreversible hash is used). We do not track your full browsing history or build a profile of the pages you visit — data is processed only at the moment you actively request a summary.

2. How we use data

Data is used solely to operate the service:

  • Tracking and enforcing the free request limit (10 filters per day).
  • Anonymous usage statistics (active users count, feature popularity).
  • Adapting Pro pricing to the user's country.

3. Pro subscription & payments

  • Payments are processed by Lemon Squeezy (our Merchant of Record).
  • During payment, Lemon Squeezy collects your email and payment details.
  • toEssence only stores your UUID and subscription status.
  • We have no access to your card details stored by Lemon Squeezy.

4. Third-party data sharing

  • OpenAI API — processes the text fragment to generate the summary. Data sent via the API is confidential, not stored for human review, and not used for AI model training.
  • Supadata — used to fetch public subtitles for YouTube videos you request a summary for.
  • Supabase — secure cloud database (EU region) storing your UUID, subscription status, anonymous usage statistics, and a temporary cache of generated summaries.
  • Lemon Squeezy — payment processing as Merchant of Record (PCI-DSS compliant).

5. Cookies & local storage

We do not use marketing or tracking cookies. UUID and interface settings are stored in browser local storage (Chrome Storage).

6. Retention & security

  • Detailed filter usage logs are automatically deleted after 90 days.
  • Aggregated anonymized statistics are retained indefinitely.
  • Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and at rest (AES-256).

7. User rights (including GDPR)

You have full control over your data. To request complete deletion of your information, email hello@toessence.net with your anonymous UUID (found in extension settings).

8. Changes to this policy

We reserve the right to update this document. In case of significant changes, users will be notified via the extension interface or on the official website.

9. Contact

Privacy questions: hello@toessence.net
