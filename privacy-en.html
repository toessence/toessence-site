Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 2026

toEssence is built with a Privacy by Design approach. We minimize data collection, enforce strict data security, and never sell, rent, or trade your data to third parties for marketing or advertising purposes. By installing and using the extension, you agree to this Privacy Policy.

1. Introduction & Chrome Web Store Compliance

To provide core functionality (generating summaries of articles and other page text), toEssence requires access to specific web page content. This data is processed strictly to deliver the service at your explicit request and is handled in full compliance with the Google Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

2. What Data We Collect & Process

We only collect data that is strictly necessary for the extension's core functionality:

  • Anonymous UUID — generated locally in your browser to identify your unique session and enforce usage limits without identifying you personally.
  • Filter counter & date — the number of filters (summary requests) used today and your local date, so we can enforce the daily limit (10 filters per day on the Free plan) and reset it at midnight.
  • Plan — your current subscription tier (free, easy, or pro).
  • Email — collected for paid users only, during plan activation. It is used to link your paid subscription to the extension and to restore it after a reinstall. We do not collect emails for free users.
  • Page URL & Text Fragment — when you explicitly request a summary (by hovering over a headline), the URL and a fragment of the article are sent to our server to generate the summary. The source article text is not stored — it is processed instantly and discarded once the summary is created. The URL and the finished summary may be temporarily cached for performance optimization.

We strictly do NOT collect or store: your name, phone number, your IP address, or country (in our databases); your full browsing history or profiles of the pages you visit; article text after the summary is generated. Data is processed only at the moment you actively request a summary.

3. How We Use Your Data

  • Operating the service, including tracking and enforcing the free request limit (10 filters per day).
  • Generating summaries of articles at your request.
  • Linking and restoring a paid subscription (for paid users, by email).
  • Generating anonymous, aggregated statistics (total user counts, feature popularity) to improve the extension.

Important: We do not use your data to personalize ads, create marketing profiles, or track you across the web.

4. Paid Subscription & Payments

Payments are securely processed by Paddle, our Merchant of Record.

  • During checkout, Paddle collects your email and payment details.
  • For paid users, we store your email address alongside your anonymous UUID. This lets you restore your subscription after reinstalling the extension and lets us link your payment to your device.
  • Code verification: to activate a plan, you enter your email, and we send a one-time code to it (valid for 10 minutes). This protects your subscription — only someone with access to your inbox can activate it. Delivery of the code email is handled by the Resend service.
  • We have no access to your credit card or financial details, which are stored only by Paddle.

5. Third-Party Data Sharing

We only share data with trusted service providers that are necessary to run the extension. We never sell your data.

  • OpenAI API — processes text fragments to generate summaries. Data transmitted via the API is confidential, encrypted, not stored for human review, and never used for AI model training.
  • Supabase — a secure cloud database (EU region) used to store your UUID, plan, email (for paid users), filter counter, and temporary summary caches.
  • Paddle — handles payment processing as a PCI-DSS compliant Merchant of Record.
  • Resend — an email delivery service, used solely to send the one-time activation code to your email.

6. Cookies & Local Storage

We do not use any marketing or tracking cookies. Your anonymous UUID and interface settings are stored locally on your device using browser local storage (Chrome Storage).

7. Data Retention & Security

  • Logs: technical request usage logs are automatically and permanently deleted after 90 days.
  • Statistics: aggregated, fully anonymized statistics are retained indefinitely to monitor product growth.
  • Security: all data is encrypted in transit using HTTPS (TLS) and at rest using AES-256 encryption.

8. User Rights (GDPR & CCPA Compliance)

You retain full control over your data. For Free users, your data is linked only to an anonymous UUID. For paid users, your data is also linked to the email you used at checkout. To request the complete deletion of your data, email us at hello@toessence.net and provide your anonymous UUID (found in the extension settings) or your subscription email.

9. Changes to This Policy

We reserve the right to update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in our practices or regulatory requirements. If significant changes are made, we will notify users through the extension interface or on our official website.

10. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or our data practices, please contact us at: hello@toessence.net
