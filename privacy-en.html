Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 2026

toEssence is built with a Privacy by Design approach. We minimize data collection, enforce strict data security, and never sell, rent, or trade your data to third parties for marketing or advertising purposes.

1. Introduction & Chrome Web Store Compliance

To provide core functionality (generating summaries of articles and YouTube videos), toEssence requires access to specific web page content. This data is processed strictly to deliver the service at your explicit request and is handled in full compliance with the Google Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

2. What Data We Collect & Process

We only collect data that is strictly necessary for the extension's core functionality. On the Free plan:

  • Anonymous UUID — generated locally in your browser to identify your unique session and enforce usage limits without identifying you personally.
  • Country — automatically determined from your IP address via a third-party geolocation service (ipapi.co) to display localized pricing. We store only the resulting country code, never your IP address.
  • Filter Type — anonymized information about the content type being processed (e.g., news, YouTube).
  • Timestamp — the date and time of your request.
  • Page URL & Text Fragment — when you explicitly request a summary (by hovering over a headline), the URL and a fragment of the article or subtitles are sent to our server. The URL and the generated summary are temporarily cached for performance; the source text fragment is processed instantly and not retained after the summary is created.

We strictly do NOT collect: your name, phone number, or your IP address; your full browsing history or profiles of the pages you visit. Data is processed only at the moment you actively request a summary.

3. How We Use Your Data

  • Operating the service, including tracking and enforcing the free request limit (10 filters per day).
  • Generating anonymous usage statistics (e.g., active user counts, feature popularity) to improve the extension.
  • Adapting Pro subscription pricing to your geographic country.

Important: We do not use your personal or browsing data to personalize ads, create marketing profiles, or track you across the web.

4. Pro Subscription & Payments

Payments are securely processed by Paddle, our Merchant of Record.

  • During checkout, Paddle collects your email and payment details.
  • For paid users, we store your email address alongside your anonymous UUID. This lets you restore your subscription after reinstalling the extension and lets us link your payment to your account.
  • We have no access to your credit card or financial details, which are stored only by Paddle.

5. Third-Party Data Sharing

We only share data with trusted service providers that are necessary to run the extension. We never sell your data.

  • OpenAI API — processes text fragments to generate summaries. Data sent via the API is confidential, encrypted, not stored for human review, and never used for AI model training.
  • Supadata — used to fetch public subtitles for YouTube videos you request a summary for.
  • Supabase — a secure cloud database (EU region) used to store your UUID, subscription status, anonymous usage statistics, and temporary summary caches.
  • Paddle — handles payment processing as a PCI-DSS compliant Merchant of Record.
  • ipapi.co — receives your IP address solely to return your country code for localized pricing. We do not store the IP address.

6. Cookies & Local Storage

We do not use any marketing or tracking cookies. Your anonymous UUID and interface settings are stored locally on your device using browser local storage (Chrome Storage).

7. Data Retention & Security

  • Logs: detailed filter usage logs are automatically and permanently deleted after 90 days.
  • Statistics: aggregated, fully anonymized statistics are retained indefinitely to monitor product growth.
  • Security: all data is encrypted in transit using HTTPS (TLS) and at rest using AES-256 encryption.

8. User Rights (GDPR & CCPA Compliance)

You retain full control over your data. For Free users, your data is linked only to an anonymous UUID. For paid users, your data is also linked to the email you used at checkout. To request the complete deletion of your data, email us at hello@toessence.net and provide your anonymous UUID (found in the extension settings) or your subscription email.

9. Changes to This Policy

We reserve the right to update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in our practices or regulatory requirements. If significant changes are made, we will notify users through the extension interface or on our official website.

10. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or our data practices, please contact us at: hello@toessence.net
